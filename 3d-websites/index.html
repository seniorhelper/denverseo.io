3D
WebGL

A 3D website uses WebGL, typically through libraries like three.js, to render interactive three-dimensional scenes in the browser. Common uses include product viewers people can rotate and zoom, virtual showrooms, immersive storytelling and games. Good 3D sites load the 3D scene on demand so the rest of the page stays fast and indexable.

Why 3D, and why now

Every phone sold today can render real-time 3D in a browser. No app, no plugin, no headset. That changed what a website can be.

Here is the psychology. People remember what they interact with far better than what they scroll past. A product you rotated yourself feels more real than one you glanced at. A room you walked into leaves a trace a banner never will.

The trap is performance. A 3D scene that blocks the page from loading destroys the speed scores and rankings you worked for. That is why we build 3D the way The Lab works: the page loads fast and fully indexable, and the 3D engine loads only when a visitor chooses to enter.

What 3D does not replace

A 3D experience is a hook, not a foundation. The page around it still needs clear text, headings, structured data and a way to call you, because search engines and AI crawlers read words, not polygons. We always pair 3D with an indexable page that explains what the experience is and why it matters.

Interactive experiences are part of a bigger strategy. Search Converts covers the marketing side in its work on interactive marketing.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What is a 3D website?

A 3D website renders interactive three-dimensional graphics in the browser using WebGL, usually through a library such as three.js. Visitors can rotate products, explore rooms or play experiences without installing anything.

Do 3D websites hurt SEO or speed?

They can if the 3D scene loads with the page. We load the 3D engine only when a visitor chooses to enter, keep the page's text and schema in plain HTML, and measure Core Web Vitals so the page stays fast and indexable.

Do 3D websites work on phones?

Yes. Modern phones support WebGL, and we design controls for touch as well as mouse. Heavy scenes are simplified on smaller devices.

How much does a 3D website cost?

It depends on the number of scenes, the detail of the models and the interactivity. A single product viewer costs far less than a full walk-in world. Our cost calculator includes 3D as an option for a planning range.

What technology do you use for 3D websites?

We build with WebGL through three.js, with custom shaders, canvas textures and lightweight models, delivered as part of a normal fast website.
