Shopify SEO is the work of making a Shopify store easy for search engines and AI shopping assistants to crawl, understand and rank. It includes controlling duplicate product and collection URLs, managing filter and sort parameters, trimming app scripts for speed, writing unique product and collection content, and adding accurate Product and Offer structured data.

Shopify is a fantastic platform to sell on. Out of the box, it also creates the same product at several URLs, lets filtered collection pages multiply, and makes it very easy to install apps that each add their own scripts to every page. None of that shows up when you look at your store. All of it shows up when Google does.

The Shopify problems we fix most

  • Duplicate URLs: products reachable through several collection paths, plus variant parameters.
  • Filter and sort bloat: thousands of near-identical collection pages competing with each other.
  • App weight: review, upsell and chat apps that pile scripts onto product pages and slow tap response.
  • Thin content: manufacturer descriptions shared by every competitor selling the same product.
  • Schema gaps: missing or inaccurate Product, Offer, shipping and return details that AI shopping assistants look for.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is Shopify good for SEO?

Shopify is a solid foundation, but its default duplicate URLs, filter pages and app scripts need attention. With those handled, Shopify stores rank very well.

How do I fix duplicate content on Shopify?

Link to canonical product URLs from collections, control filter and sort parameters, and make sure every product has a single canonical URL with unique content.

Do Shopify apps slow down my store?

Many do. Each app can add scripts to every page. Removing unused apps and replacing heavy ones often produces the biggest speed gains.

How much do Shopify SEO services cost?

It depends on catalog size and what needs fixing. Most stores start with a technical audit so the budget goes to the fixes that matter most.
