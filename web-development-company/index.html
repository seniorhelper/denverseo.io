Same look. Only one gets found.

A web development company plans, codes, tests and launches websites and web applications. The best ones also build in search engine optimization, structured data, accessibility, security and speed from the first line of code, so the site can be found and can convert once people arrive.

Here is a question worth sitting with. If your site vanished tomorrow, how many customers would notice?

For most businesses, the honest answer is not many. The site exists. It looks fine. It just does not do anything. It does not rank, it does not answer questions, and it does not make the phone ring.

That is not a design problem. It is an engineering problem. And it is the one we solve.

What makes our builds different

We hand-code. No page builders stacking plugins on plugins, no bloated themes shipping thirty scripts you never asked for. Every page is written for three readers at once: the person looking at it, the search engine crawling it, and the AI assistant deciding whether to quote it.

Search-first architecture

URL structure, navigation and internal links planned before design, so authority flows to the pages that earn money.

Structured data built in

Organization, Service, FAQ, Product and Breadcrumb schema written into the templates, not bolted on later by a plugin.

Fast by default

Static-first builds, optimized images, minimal JavaScript. Core Web Vitals targets are part of the spec, not a wish.

Answers AI can quote

Every key page opens with a direct, plain-language answer. That is what answer engines lift into their responses.

Conversion paths

One clear primary action per page, click-to-call on mobile, short forms with bot protection that does not annoy humans.

Accessible and secure

Semantic HTML, labeled forms, contrast that passes WCAG checks, HTTPS everywhere and no plugin attack surface.

How a build runs

From blank page to live site

Every project follows the same spine. What changes is the size of each step.

  1. Discovery. We learn what the site must make happen: calls, bookings, quotes or sales. That single answer shapes every decision after it.
  2. Search map. Keyword and question research decides which pages exist and what each one targets, so no two pages fight each other.
  3. Design in code. We design directly in the browser, so what you approve is what ships, at real speed on a real phone.
  4. Build and wire. Templates, schema, forms, tracking, and any tools or bots the site needs.
  5. Test. Desktop and mobile, every page, every form, every link. Structured data validated. Speed measured.
  6. Launch and watch. Search Console, indexing, and the first weeks of real behavior. Then we improve what the data shows.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What does a web development company do?

A web development company plans, designs, codes, tests and launches websites and web applications. A good one also builds in SEO, speed, accessibility, security and conversion paths so the site produces business results, not just a nice look.

How much does a custom website cost?

It depends on scope: page count, features, integrations and how much is custom code. Simple brochure sites cost far less than ecommerce or web applications. Use our free website cost calculator for a planning range, then talk to us for a real quote.

Do you only build expensive websites?

No. We build everything from simple, fast sites to large custom platforms, and our DIY builder plans start at $5.99 a month. The $30,000 to $50,000 subscribe-to-own build is one option for businesses we believe in, not our only offer.

Do you use WordPress or page builders?

We hand-code most builds for speed and control, and we also build on WordPress and Shopify when that is the right fit for the client. We avoid heavy page builders because they slow sites down and add security risk.

Do you work with businesses outside Denver?

Yes. We are headquartered in Denver and build websites for businesses across the United States and worldwide.

Will my new website rank on Google?

No honest developer can guarantee rankings. What we can do is remove every technical barrier, structure the site around real searches, and build pages that deserve to rank. The rest depends on competition, content and authority over time.
