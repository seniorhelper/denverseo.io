Features

Website development cost is mainly the number of hours a project takes multiplied by the rate of the people doing the work. Hours are driven by site type, page count, custom features, content creation and integrations. A simple brochure site needs far fewer hours than an ecommerce store or web application.

How to use this estimate

The hour ranges come from typical scopes for each type of build. They are meant for planning and for sanity-checking quotes, not as a price. If one quote is far below the low end, ask what is being left out: content, SEO, testing and launch support are the usual casualties. If one is far above, ask what the extra hours buy.

What makes websites expensive

  • Content. Writing good pages takes longer than coding them. Many projects stall waiting on content.
  • Custom functionality. Calculators, portals, booking and integrations add real engineering time.
  • Revisions without a plan. Changing direction mid-build is the fastest way to double hours.
  • Migration. Moving an existing site without losing rankings takes careful mapping and redirects.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

How much does it cost to build a website?

Costs range from a few hundred dollars for a DIY builder site to tens of thousands or more for custom ecommerce sites and web applications. The biggest factors are site type, page count, custom features, content and the rate of the team.

Why do website quotes vary so much?

Quotes differ in what they include. Low quotes often leave out copywriting, SEO setup, testing and launch support, or rely on templates. Higher quotes may include strategy, custom code and ongoing optimization. Always compare line by line.

Is this calculator a quote?

No. It produces a planning range from typical hour estimates. A real quote requires understanding your goals, content and integrations.

Can I pay for a website monthly?

Yes. Some agencies, including ours for qualifying businesses, offer subscribe-to-own plans where you pay monthly and can buy the site later.
