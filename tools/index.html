These free tools check how search engines, AI crawlers and AI agents read a website: whether structured data is valid, which AI bots robots.txt allows, how machine-readable a page is, and how internal links connect pages. They run entirely in the browser.

Schema Markup Validator

Paste JSON-LD. Get errors, warnings, required properties by type, and a rotating 3D graph of your entities.

AI Crawler Checker

Paste robots.txt. See whether GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and 14 more can read any path, and which rule decided it.

llms.txt Generator

Build a valid llms.txt file that points AI tools to your most important pages. Copy or download.

Machine-Readability Analyzer

Paste HTML. Get a score for headings, schema, quotable sentences, alt text and agent-ready forms.

Website Development Cost Calculator

Pick a site type, pages and features. Get planning hours and a cost range at your chosen rate.

Internal Link Graph

List your links. Find orphan pages, buried pages and dead ends in a 3D map.
