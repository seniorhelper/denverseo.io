Website maintenance services keep a website secure, fast, functional and visible in search after launch. That includes software and plugin updates, backups, uptime and form monitoring, broken link fixes, speed checks, SSL renewals, content updates and technical SEO health checks.

Think about your car. You would never drive it for three years without an oil change and then act surprised when the engine seizes.

Websites work the same way, except the warning lights are invisible. There is no dashboard telling you the booking form has been failing for six weeks. You find out when a customer mentions they tried to reach you and gave up.

What maintenance actually covers

Updates and patches

CMS core, themes, plugins and dependencies kept current, tested before they go live, rolled back if they break something.

Backups you can restore

Automated, off-site, versioned backups. And the part most people skip: we actually test restoring them.

Uptime and form monitoring

We check that the site is up and that your forms really deliver, because a silent form failure is lost revenue.

Speed and Core Web Vitals

Regular checks against Google's thresholds, and fixes when new content or scripts slow things down.

SEO health

Index coverage, crawl errors, broken links, redirects and structured data watched in Search Console.

Content updates

Hours, prices, team, offers and seasonal pages changed quickly, so the site tells the truth.

Monthly, quarterly, yearly

Not everything needs attention every week. A sensible plan splits the work by rhythm:

  • Monthly: updates, backups, uptime and form checks, speed spot checks, Search Console review.
  • Quarterly: full link audit, structured data validation, content accuracy review, security scan.
  • Yearly: technical SEO audit, accessibility review, design and conversion refresh, hosting and license review.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What is included in website maintenance?

Typically software and plugin updates, backups, security monitoring, uptime and form checks, speed monitoring, broken link fixes, SSL renewals, small content changes and technical SEO health checks. Plans differ, so always ask for the exact list.

How often should a website be maintained?

Core tasks like updates, backups and form checks should happen monthly or more often. Deeper reviews such as link audits and structured data checks work well quarterly, with a full technical and accessibility audit yearly.

Does website maintenance help SEO?

Yes. Maintenance prevents the slow decay that hurts rankings: broken links, slower pages, crawl errors, outdated content and security problems. It keeps the technical foundation that SEO depends on intact.

Do static websites need maintenance?

Less than CMS sites, because there are no plugins or databases to patch. They still need content updates, link checks, speed checks, form testing and search monitoring.

Can you maintain a site you did not build?

Usually, yes. We start with an audit to understand how it was built, what is fragile and what needs fixing before regular maintenance begins.
