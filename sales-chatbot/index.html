A sales chatbot is a website assistant built to move visitors toward a purchase or appointment. Unlike a generic support bot, it is trained on the business's services, pricing approach and common objections, asks qualifying questions, and hands warm leads to a person by phone, form or booking link.

Look to the right edge of this page. That is Root, our own site guide. He is not a person and he says so if you ask. What he does is what a good first salesperson does: listens, asks one useful question at a time, and knows when to hand you to a human.

That is the model we build for businesses, trained on their services instead of ours.

Built on a sales playbook, not a script

Our founder spent years as a sales manager for multi-million-dollar plumbing companies and national remodeling companies, running the teams that took the calls. The bots we build follow the same principles those teams did:

  • Permission first. The bot asks before it pitches. People buy more from someone who asks than someone who tells.
  • One question at a time. Situation, problem, impact, then the next step. No interrogation forms dressed up as chat.
  • Honest trial closes. "Would it help to have someone look at this?" beats "BUY NOW" every time.
  • Hand-off fast. When the lead is warm, the bot gets out of the way and connects them to your phone.
  • No fake promises. It never guarantees results, invents prices or pretends to be human.

What a sales chatbot does for you

Answers after hours

Nights, weekends and holidays, the moments when research happens and competitors are closed.

Qualifies honestly

Asks the questions your best salesperson would, so your team only calls the leads worth calling.

Handles common objections

Price, timing, trust, "I need to think about it." Trained responses that respect the buyer.

Books the next step

Callback requests, estimate bookings, or a tap to call your phone number.

Learns your pages

Page-aware openers: the bot on your pricing page talks differently than the one on your blog.

Stays out of the way

Collapses politely, remembers when a visitor closed it, and never covers your call buttons.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What is a sales chatbot?

A sales chatbot is a website assistant designed to move visitors toward a sale or appointment. It answers questions about your services, asks qualifying questions, handles common objections and hands warm leads to your team.

Is a sales chatbot the same as an AI chatbot?

Not always. Some sales chatbots are rule-based and follow a designed conversation, like Root on this site. Others use large language models trained on a knowledge base. The right choice depends on how complex your questions are and how much control you need.

Will a chatbot annoy my visitors?

A badly built one will. Good sales chatbots open once, respect a close, remember that choice across pages and never cover call buttons or forms. They ask permission instead of pushing.

Can a chatbot book appointments?

Yes. A sales chatbot can collect callback requests, link to a booking calendar, or connect to your phone line, depending on how your business schedules work.

How long does it take to build a sales chatbot?

A focused rule-based bot trained on your core services can be built quickly. Knowledge-base bots that cover large catalogs or many services take longer, mostly because the content has to be gathered and verified.
